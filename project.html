THE PROJECT

Chiang Mai & Beyond
Biodiversity Project

A long-term personal project exploring the biodiversity of Chiang Mai and surrounding areas through photography, fieldwork, scientific research, and data.

01 — PURPOSE

Why this project exists

Chiang Mai and beyond areas contains a remarkable variety of environments, from urban areas and gardens to agricultural land, rivers, forests, and mountains.

This project aims to document the organisms living within these environments and explore how biodiversity changes between different habitats.

02 — WHAT I DOCUMENT

Species

Plants, birds, mammals, insects, and other organisms observed during fieldwork.

Habitats

Forests, mountains, rivers, gardens, agricultural areas, and urban environments.

Observations

Photographs, dates, approximate locations, habitat information, and field notes.

03 — FIELDWORK

From observation to data.

Field observations will be collected during visits to different natural areas around Chiang Mai and beyond.

Each observation can be organized into a structured record, allowing photographs and field notes to become useful biodiversity data.

04 — DATA

Building a biodiversity record.

Records may include species name, date, approximate location, habitat, elevation, photograph, identification source, and additional observations.

Over time, these records can be used to explore seasonal changes, habitat differences, and patterns in species observations.

05 — SOURCES

Catalogue of Life

Taxonomic and species reference information.

GBIF

Global biodiversity occurrence data.

iNaturalist

Biodiversity observations and species identification.

LOOKING AHEAD

A project that grows over time.

The project will gradually develop through continued fieldwork, photography, scientific research, mapping, and data analysis.
